1. What it does

review-invoice (no PII) validates totals, vendor allowlist, spending limits. approve-payment charges via Stripe test mode with PII resolved host-side through http-with-placeholders — bank keys never enter prompts, logs, or WASM memory. Secrets live in private KV z:<tid>:secrets; single egress host; every dispatch lands in the audit log. Finance teams approve invoices without pasting sensitive data into AI context.

2. Why it is maintainable

  • Thin TEE: 2 functions, ~200 lines Rust, same envelope + WIT pattern as reference.
  • Policy in one config.ts (limits, allowlist, hosts) — no Rust change for policy tweaks.
  • Single-version pin (0.4.1), recorded contract_id, re-grant ACLs on bump.
  • Repeatable: docs/LOCAL_TEST.md; weekly ops ~30 min (credits, logs, delegation windows, SDK changelog).
  • Stack: TypeScript/Node/Express/Postgres-mirror — no exotic deps.
